The premise

Growth happens everywhere. Every community needs space to self-actuate.

People grow in places — the ground beneath them, the platforms they gather on, and increasingly the environments that are generated around them. Each kind of space shapes how a person or a community develops.

selfdriven.space is a reference for those spaces: the physical, the virtual and the composed, and how they come to be activated.

Space is claimed by people — individually and together.

How space is activated.

A space becomes a place for growth when someone claims it. That can begin with one person's agency, or with a community's collective need.

  1. 01 · Self-Initiated

    Born from individual agency

    Spaces born from individual agency — a person or group decides to create, claim or activate an environment for their own growth and purpose.

    Activated by collective need
